WHERE THE WORK HAPPENS

On-site or in the workshop.

Fitted woodwork & localized care

On-site work may suit doors, staircases and some surface repairs. Access, protection, ventilation and drying requirements are assessed first.

Portable furniture & involved restoration

Workshop conditions may suit detailed repairs and more substantial refinishing. Handling and transport are discussed during assessment.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

A clearer way to choose.

Do I need to know which finish is on my furniture?+

No. Share what you know, but the existing surface should be assessed before treatment is proposed.

Can every piece be French polished?+

No. Suitability depends on the material, existing finish and use. Shellac is sensitive to water, heat and alcohol.

Will a repair completely remove every mark?+

Not always. Damage depth and finish condition influence what can be achieved. Limitations should be discussed before agreeing work.
