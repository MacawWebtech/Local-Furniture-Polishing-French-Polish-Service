WHAT TO LOOK FOR

A comparison deserves context.

The same piece

Photograph the same surface from a similar angle, with consistent lighting, to show an honest.

The agreed work

Explain the starting condition, treatment and chosen finish alongside the photographs.

The retained character

Not every restoration aims for a brand-new appearance. Original material and patina can be worth preserving.
