WHAT SHAPES YOUR QUOTE

The piece determines the work.

Size & construction

Surface area, profiles, joints, veneers and details influence preparation and handling.

Material & finish

Wood type, finish compatibility and contamination determine suitable treatment.

Condition & damage

Damage severity and previous repairs affect whether local repair or broader work is appropriate.

Preparation

Cleaning, repair, careful sanding where suitable and tone trials take different amounts of time.

Finish selection

Shellac, varnish and other systems have different application, drying and care requirements.

On-site or workshop

Access, protection, transport and working conditions influence the agreed scope.

BEFORE YOU AGREE

A quotation should explain the details.

The scope

Ask which surfaces and repairs are included, what will be retained and what result is proposed.

The logistics

Confirm access, collection or transport, working arrangements and likely timing.

The finish & care

Agree finish type and sheen, curing requirements and the aftercare you will need.

PRICING QUESTIONS

Make the next step an informed one.

Are these prices fixed?+

No. Prices are indicative. A final quotation requires an agreed assessment and scope.

Why can similar furniture cost different amounts?+

Existing finish, construction, damage and preparation can differ even when two pieces look similar in size.

Does a bigger restoration mean a better result?+

Not always. Localized work or retaining sound original material may be more appropriate than extensive refinishing.

Are transport, tax and extra repairs included?+

These sample prices do not define those charges. Confirm all applicable costs and inclusions in an actual written quotation.

Can I book using the calculator?+

No. Use the assessment form to prepare your request. Your appointment requires confirmation.
