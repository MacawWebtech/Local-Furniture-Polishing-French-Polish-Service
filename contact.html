BEFORE YOU GET IN TOUCH

A few useful answers.

What information should I include?+

Describe the furniture, its size, condition and intended use. Note any known finish or earlier repair.

Can you quote from photographs?+

Photographs can help with an initial discussion. A firm scope and price may require an in-person inspection.

Can the work happen at my home?+

Some work may be suitable on-site. Access, ventilation, preparation and the chosen finishing method determine what is practical.

Should I bring the piece to the workshop?+

Arrange the visit first. Confirm handling, transport and storage requirements before moving the furniture.
